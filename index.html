Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026

16–18 September 2026

Speke Resort Munyonyo, Kampala, Uganda

Welcome to the Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026

The Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026 is a platform that brings together coffee farmers, exporters, processors, investors and other industry professionals from Uganda and around the world. The event aims to celebrate Uganda's coffee industry and showcase opportunities within the global coffee market.

Organised by the Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries, the three-day event will feature exhibitions, expert discussions, networking opportunities and presentations on coffee production, processing, marketing and innovation.
